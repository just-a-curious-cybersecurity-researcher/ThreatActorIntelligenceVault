## Review Coverage

Research covered direct malware analyses, incident response, official national reporting, vendor actor tracking, extortion trackers, blockchain analysis and official sanctions sources. Search also examined broad profiles proposing exact CVEs; entries without a Lynx-specific intrusion chain were moved to the qualified/disputed table rather than treated as observed exploitation.

Tracker values were captured as dated snapshots. No actor portal was authenticated to, no stolen data was accessed and no negotiation contact was attempted.
