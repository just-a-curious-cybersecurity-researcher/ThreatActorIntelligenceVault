# The Gentlemen — Attribution

**Presentation reviewed:** 2026-09-17.

## Evidence Classes and Confidence

**High Confidence** means a publisher directly identifies a sample or observed behavior. **Moderate Confidence** covers vendor assessments and scoped campaign associations. Tracker entries and recruitment claims are retained as those evidence types. A vendor label is not a verified civil identity.

| Label | Source and scope | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| The Gentlemen / Gentlemen | Ransomware and RaaS branding | The singular “The Gentleman” in the supplied notes is a spelling variant. |
| Storm-2697 | Microsoft's operator tracking label | Do not assign all affiliates or every shared tool to one operator. |
| zeta88 / hastalamuerte | Check Point associates these handles with administration | Pseudonyms and an assessed relationship, not legal identification. |

## Geographic Nexus

Reported avoidance of Russia and CIS targets, and Russian-language operational material, support a Russian-speaking ecosystem assessment. They do not independently establish residence, nationality or state sponsorship.

Check Point links the administrator's role to shared Tox identifiers and the reviewed internal conversations. Its named-operator/chat population and Unit 42's larger approximate operator estimate are different visibility sets, not a stable headcount. Preserve the report date and definition rather than choosing one number as the organization's verified size.

Microsoft and Unit 42 report a BreachForums recruitment partnership in May 2026. This is an affiliate-recruitment relationship; it does not make every marketplace user a ransomware affiliate.

## Decision Use

Use sample identity, contact identifiers and incident chronology together. The broad supplied YARA brand match is insufficient to identify a locker, and legitimate tooling such as AnyDesk or PowerRun does not establish actor attribution.

## Official Organizational Evidence and Limits

COLCERT's campaign advisory is official defensive reporting. Its proposed Qilin split, support relationships and identity claims are qualified within the advisory itself. Those claims do not independently prove the entire organizational graph. Its handling of pseudonyms and alleged organizational links is not a criminal indictment or independent confirmation of those identities.
