# The Gentlemen — Blockchain and Financial Intelligence

**Presentation reviewed:** 2026-09-17.

## Overview

Financial motivation and double extortion are established operational context. Recruitment terms, negotiation evidence and blockchain attribution answer different questions and are kept separate.

## Blockchain Attribution and Visibility Limitations

TRM reports USD 99.6K in The Gentlemen-attributed flows to AudiA6. This is a provider's attributed service flow, not the group's total income or a verified payment by every listed victim.

## Financial Evidence Timeline

Check Point's May 2026 analysis describes advertised affiliate terms, payment discussions and a negotiation screenshot. TRM's AudiA6 reporting supplies an independent financial-service association. The source register records both. The chat article discusses a roughly USD 250,000 demand reduced to USD 190,000; its narrative presents a successful negotiation, while the reproduced screenshot is not an independently verified transaction record. This amount must not be added to the AudiA6 flow as if both measured the same financial population.

## Financial Relationships

The advertised division is 90% for affiliates and 10% for the operator. An advertised split is not proof that a particular transaction was divided that way. Internal discussions reference Bitcoin payments and non-custodial wallet software; application names are not wallet addresses. The forum allegation of about USD 48,000 in unpaid Qilin affiliate commission is a reported dispute supporting the lineage discussion, not an attributed Gentlemen wallet balance.

## Analyst Note

Keep payment demands, agreements, settled transactions and downstream service deposits distinct. A shared financial intermediary does not establish shared leadership with Akira or Qilin.

## Independent Corroboration and Financial-Service Disruption

The TRM finding adds on-chain provider evidence to the chat-derived financial account. Its reported AudiA6 link is a service relationship. The associated disruption does not demonstrate termination of this ransomware program.

## Regulatory and Judicial Review

The source review includes Treasury/OFAC and financial-provider searches. Only the supported AudiA6 context is retained here; an intermediary investigation must not be turned into a designation of every customer.
