## Review Coverage

The supplied dossier is the baseline collection input. Named publishers without a specific retrievable article were treated as leads, not as sources silently marked reviewed. The [source-review register](intelligence/source-review.md) records substantive corrections and disposition.

Primary incident reports, reverse engineering, government reporting and financial research carry different evidentiary roles. Secondary summaries do not count as independent corroboration of their underlying reports. Neither this source list nor a name search is a complete sanctions-screening result.

The TIREDBIKE alias in the supplied notes was not independently established by the retrieved Google article and is not promoted into the actor alias table. Tracker counts, extra mirrors, generic note variants and hashes without inspected provenance were likewise kept out of confirmed indicator sets.
