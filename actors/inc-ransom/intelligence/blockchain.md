# INC Ransom — Blockchain and Financial Intelligence

**Presentation reviewed:** 2026-09-22.

## Overview

INC is financially motivated and uses encryption and disclosure pressure to obtain payment. The current official organizational account separates affiliate intrusion activity from the core's extortion and payment role. A ransom demand, a completed payment and net operator revenue are different measurements.

The most useful public financial evidence is the relationship between payment flows and operational roles, rather than a presumed single INC treasury.

## Blockchain Attribution and Visibility Limitations

Treat a negotiation address as evidence for its specific incident and date. The initial recipient may represent a collection role; later recipients may be affiliates, intermediaries or services. Control of a private key cannot be inferred solely from receiving funds.

For Bitcoin, change outputs, shared service deposits and consolidation can complicate cluster interpretation. A transaction hash identifies a transfer, whereas an address identifies a destination or script representation. Neither is interchangeable with a person or an extortion brand.

This dossier keeps address-level indicators separate from provider-level cluster findings in the [address-scope register](../iocs/blockchain-addresses.md). Aggregate findings are not expanded into guessed addresses.

## Financial Evidence Timeline

### 2025-02-05 — INC and Lynx

Chainalysis reports similar on-chain behavior linking INC and Lynx. This supplies a financial observation alongside the technical relationship. It does not establish that all Lynx payments were INC revenue or that an entire operation changed name on one date.

### 2026-04-08 — Shared laundering cluster

TRM describes almost 200 addresses spanning INC, Lynx, Sinobi and info@ranshelp, with non-VPN access associated with Iran. Of 15 traced payments, nine flows were associated with administrator activity and six with affiliates. Its typical 80/20 split is a RaaS analytical pattern, not a published INC-wide contract.

The same report assigns HashCr4cker and Bearhost payments specifically to Sinobi-linked addresses. Those service relationships are not transferred to INC by association.

### 2026-05-19 — Payments to a malware-signing service

Microsoft's cryptocurrency analysis links Fox Tempest to affiliates delivering INC and other ransomware families. Fox Tempest supplied malware-signing services; it is not described as the INC treasury. The report's proceeds figure spans the investigated service ecosystem and must not be presented as INC revenue.

This adds a supplier relationship to the existing cross-brand financial picture. Payments for enabling services and ransom receipts have different economic roles, even when both appear in an investigator's transaction graph.

## Financial Relationships

The evidence supports three distinct relationships: victim-to-extortion collection, separation of administrator and affiliate proceeds, and reuse of financial infrastructure across brands. Shared infrastructure can reflect a laundering provider or a participant using several ransomware services.

A percentage split is a hypothesis to test against transactions and case context. It is not enough to label the larger output “affiliate” automatically: fees, change, aggregation and multiple payments can produce misleading amounts.

The same separation applies to cash-out services. Funds reaching an exchange deposit indicate service use; they do not establish ownership of the exchange or every address in its cluster.

## Analyst Note

For an incident, retain the original payment instruction, network, address, amount, timestamp, transaction identifier and the source linking it to the negotiation. Keep victim identifiers and correspondence in the authorized case system rather than publishing sensitive negotiation material.

Preserve whether an amount is demanded, transferred, recovered or estimated. Do not add a leak-site victim count to a payment count, or combine overlapping provider estimates into a fictional revenue total.

## Independent Corroboration and Financial-Service Disruption

Chainalysis' financial relationship and Unit 42's code comparison support different aspects of the INC/Lynx relationship. They should be assessed together while preserving their methods and scope.

Financial-service infrastructure offers an investigative pivot when a case has an attributable transaction. Provider records and lawful preservation can help connect payment infrastructure to an account. The public cluster description itself is not a transaction graph reproduced by this repository.

## Regulatory and Judicial Review

Sanctions references attached to other dossiers remain associated with their named subjects. LockBit member designations, exchange sanctions and general ransomware advisories do not become an INC designation merely because the ecosystem overlaps.

The official Tonga account concerns exfiltration infrastructure. It does not identify a sanctioned INC wallet or establish treasury ownership. A dated name search is also not a substitute for screening an actual payment counterparty and address against current official records.
