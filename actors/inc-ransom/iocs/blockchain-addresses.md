## Address Attribution and Scope

The financial evidence retained here is at the provider-cluster level. Its scope and observations are recorded in [blockchain intelligence](../intelligence/blockchain.md); no inferred address rows are generated from cluster counts or diagrams.

Only complete addresses tied to a documented incident, named role or official entry qualify for this register. A payment demand in BTC does not reveal its destination.

## Downstream and Regulatory Context

Cross-brand financial relationships do not transfer ownership of every downstream address to INC. Official entries belonging to other groups remain in their own dossiers. The centralized [source register](../References.md) distinguishes incident reporting, financial analysis and the scope of official attribution.
