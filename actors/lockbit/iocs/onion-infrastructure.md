### Historical control changes

Cronos seized infrastructure in 2024. A pre-seizure contact URL can later identify a law-enforcement-controlled site. Tracking names across versions therefore requires a time interval and role, rather than an assumption that a familiar prefix proves continuing actor control.

The supplied malformed legacy analytics onion is omitted instead of being repaired by guesswork. Complete v3 names are checksum-validated locally.
