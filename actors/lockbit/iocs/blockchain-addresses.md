## Address Attribution and Scope

The first table contains eleven identifiers officially attributed to three individuals: ten Bitcoin addresses and one Ethereum address. The possible-impostor entry is a separate extortion-note artifact. A personal identifier does not assign every receipt to a particular LockBit incident, and a published demand address does not establish payment.

Dates identify the public designation or indicator report, not first_seen/last_seen transactions. No current balances are asserted. The lower-case SegWit spelling follows OFAC; Chainalysis's mixed-case transcription of one address is not copied. The Ethereum entry does not demonstrate a Bitcoin-to-Ethereum laundering route.

The tens of thousands of panel records discussed in the financial analysis are a different population. This register does not relabel unused panel destinations as confirmed victim payments or represent the officially listed subset as the complete LockBit address cluster.
