## Publication Provenance

Indicators are separated into encryptor artifacts, DragonForce/Hackledorb supporting malware, incident infrastructure and lower-confidence aggregator values. Scattered Spider indicators are included only when a reviewed source directly connects them to a DragonForce-ending case. Report publication dates are not presented as `first_seen` or `last_seen`.

Infrastructure and exact hashes are volatile. A match supports investigation in its documented scope; absence does not exclude rebuilt affiliate payloads. Original URLs are centralized in [References](../References.md).
