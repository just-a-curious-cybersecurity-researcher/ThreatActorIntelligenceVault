## Review Coverage

Collection prioritizes direct incident response, reverse engineering, corporate disclosures, regulatory filings and dated trackers. Vendor actor cards and aggregators are retained for leads and discrepancies. Search coverage included CrowdStrike, Mandiant/Google, Microsoft, Fortinet, Check Point, Trend Micro, Unit 42, SentinelOne, Sophos, Secureworks, Sygnia, Triskele Labs, CISA, NCSC-UK, ACSC, Europol, DOJ, OFAC, UK OFSI, the EU, TRM Labs, Chainalysis and Elliptic.

No dedicated public CrowdStrike adversary, Mandiant UNC actor, Microsoft Storm actor or MITRE group/software object for SafePay was located. No primary source exposed an operator identity, public affiliate panel, Linux/ESXi locker sample, victim-specific payment wallet, public decryptor weakness or direct SafePay sanctions action. These searches are evidence gaps, not proof that the corresponding capability or relationship cannot exist.

Tracker figures were captured on 2026-09-24 and can change. RansomLook and BreachSense differ in total and last-seen date because of parser health, source coverage, timestamps and deduplication. DLS posts are claims, not independently confirmed intrusions.

## Executable Analysis Provenance

The early-build analysis is based principally on SP006 and SP008. The revised-build analysis is based principally on SP007 and cross-checked against SP009, SP010 and SP013. SP011/SP012 support IOC reconciliation but are not used to invent unreported binary internals.

The 65-byte footer, `CryptGenRandom`, ChaCha20-only description and language guard belong to the NCC/Huntress-era sample set. The 80-byte footer, AES-NI selection, `RtlGenRandom`, SHA-512 derivation and omitted language guard belong to DCSO's later analyzed build. These differences are kept sample-scoped. No ransomware binary was downloaded or executed while preparing the dossier.
